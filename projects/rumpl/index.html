About the project

Rumpl makes outdoor blankets and comfort gear, from blankets and sleep systems to mats and towels, in technical materials with bold, colourful design for campsites, beaches, road trips and the sofa.

What it needed

The catalogue never stands still: seasonal drops and campaigns arrive constantly, many products come in a wide matrix of sizes, prints and materials, and the store has to stay quick and clear to browse on a phone while merchandising changes around it.

The work

Collection and product templates designed to carry heavy variant ranges, campaign sections the team can compose for each drop, and an image-loading approach suited to a large, image-led catalogue.

The experience

Shoppers move from clear categories (blankets, sleep systems, mats, towels and accessories) into product pages where every variant stays easy to compare and choose.
