Collaboration model

Three ways to work together.

Pick the shape that fits the project; we agree it in writing before anything starts.

  1. Collaboration model · 01 / 03 01

    Behind the scenes

    We work under your brand and inside your process. The client relationship stays yours; we deliver to it.

  2. Collaboration model · 02 / 03 02

    Alongside your team

    We join as an extra pair of hands on design or build, in your tools, your rituals and your timeline.

  3. Collaboration model · 03 / 03 03

    End to end

    We take a defined piece from brief to launch, then hand it back documented so your team can carry it on.

  1. How it runs · 01 / 03 01

    Scope

    A short conversation, then a written scope with clear milestones and an honest timeline.

  2. How it runs · 02 / 03 02

    Build

    Work in agreed milestones with regular, async-friendly updates and nothing hidden until launch week.

  3. How it runs · 03 / 03 03

    Handoff

    Documented code, files and decisions, so your team owns the work after we step back.
