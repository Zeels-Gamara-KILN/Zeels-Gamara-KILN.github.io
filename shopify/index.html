What we do with Shopify

Shopify is where brand meets operations: what a customer sees has to work with what the team runs every day in the admin.

KILN. works at that seam, through the studio and its technical development partner network: designing the storefront experience and building it in the theme itself, from catalogue structure and product pages to configurators, bundles and subscriptions, so the store feels like the brand and the people running it keep control of their content, products and campaigns.

Capabilities

Shopify, end to end.

Design and development across the whole storefront, from the first collection page to the checkout hand-off.

  1. 01

    Custom storefronts

    Storefronts designed and built around the brand and its catalogue, rather than adapted from a template.

  2. 02

    Shopify theme development

    Online Store 2.0 themes with reusable sections, blocks and metafield-driven content that teams can extend.

  3. 03

    Shopify Markets

    Multi-market stores with market-aware pricing, currencies and languages.

  4. 04

    Responsive implementation

    Built for phones first and checked across real devices, from collection grids to checkout hand-off.

  5. 05

    Conversion-focused UX

    Clear buying paths: collection pages, product galleries, variant choice and cart.

  6. 06

    Integrations

    Subscriptions, email and reviews (such as Recharge and Klaviyo) connected cleanly in the theme.

  7. 07

    E-commerce experience design

    Product storytelling, merchandising and campaign sections, designed with the build in mind.

  8. 08

    Custom functionality

    Configurators, bundles and gifting flows built into the theme, not bolted on.

  9. 09

    Performance & accessibility

    Stable, fast-loading pages and semantic, keyboard-friendly commerce UI.

  10. 10

    Launch & handoff

    Documented code and QA before launch, so teams can carry the store forward.
